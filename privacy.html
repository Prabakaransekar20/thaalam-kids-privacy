Privacy Policy for Thaalam Kids

100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Thaalam Kids ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and parents who use our mobile application Thaalam Kids (Package Name: com.thaalam.kids).

1. Summary — Zero Data Collection

Thaalam Kids is designed to be a safe, offline, educational app for children (ages 2 to 8).

  • We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or device data.
  • We do NOT use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do NOT require internet access.
  • We do NOT request any Android device permissions.

2. Information Collection and Use

Personal Data: We do not collect any personal data, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, photos, or contact information.

Device Data: We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, or hardware signatures.

Purchases: Thaalam Kids contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

3. App Permissions

Thaalam Kids is designed with a strict zero-permission architecture. The app requires no system permissions (no INTERNET, CAMERA, MICROPHONE, LOCATION, or STORAGE permissions). Because the app operates completely offline, no data ever leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Thaalam Kids does not integrate with any third-party services, including advertising networks, analytics tools, social media platforms, or crash-reporting services.

5. On-Device Storage

To preserve child progress (unlocked stickers, completed levels, and stars), Thaalam Kids saves progress locally on your device using Android local storage (DataStore). This data is stored purely on the local device, is inaccessible to other apps, and is never transmitted to any remote server or external party.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our app is built specifically for children and fully complies with:

  • The Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • EU General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K)
  • Google Play Families Policy
